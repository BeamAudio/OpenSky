# Airport scenery sources in Spain

Use the same sources for existing and newly built regions:

* [IGN PNOA current orthophotos](https://pnoa.ign.es/pnoa-imagen/visualizadores-y-servicios-web) provide nationwide aerial imagery through WMS/WMTS, and [downloadable imagery](https://pnoa.ign.es/web/portal/pnoa-imagen/ortofotos-pnoa-maxima-actualidad). The [national catalogue](https://datos.gob.es/es/catalogo/e00125901-spaignpnoama) lists CC BY 4.0. Check imagery dates and attribution for each pack. Aerial orthophotos are a ground image; they do not by themselves provide a complete 3D model.
* [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) supplies mapped runway, taxiway, apron, building and road geometry under ODbL. Coverage, surface tags and detail vary by location. Missing tags must remain unknown; avoid inventing a surface.
* [OurAirports](https://ourairports.com/data/) provides public-domain airport and runway records, with variable completeness. Reconcile duplicate records and compare with the imagery and airport operator where possible.
* [ENAIRE AIP](https://aip.enaire.es/) is the official reference for Spanish aeronautical charts. Its [chart index](https://aip.enaire.es/AIP/contenido_AIP/GEN/LE_GEN_3_2_en.html) describes aerodrome and ground movement charts where available. Use it for reference; review applicable reuse terms before distributing ENAIRE material in a pack. Small airfields may have no published detailed ground chart.

For Castejón de Sos, the [municipality](https://www.xn--castejndesos-5hb.es/campo-de-vuelo) describes a 450 × 15 m grass strip and hangars. This is a reference for checking the general pipeline, not a substitute for the sources at other airports.
